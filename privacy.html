LumiSprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026
App: LumiSprout (Android), version 1.0
Provider: Avonique Labs, Al Warsan, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (“we”, “us”)
Contact: avoniquelabs@gmail.com

1. Summary

LumiSprout is an offline learning app for children aged 3–8. We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal information. Everything the app creates — child profiles, learning progress, drawings and settings — stays only on the device where it was created. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

2. Information stored on your device (not collected by us)

To work, the app saves the following only on the device, in the app's private storage:

InformationPurposeWho can see it
Child nickname (optional), age (3–8), chosen avatar, learning language and focus areasShow age-appropriate activities and personalise the experienceAnyone using the device; parent area protected by a PIN
Activities played, answers correct on the first try, hints used, time spent, stars and badgesRecommend activities, show progress to the parent, grow the child's gardenSame as above

We never ask for a child's full name, date of birth, photo, voice, school, location, contact details or any device identifier.

3. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs that send data.
  • No chat, social features, public profiles or links to external websites from children's screens.
  • No camera, microphone or location access. Children's voices are never recorded.
  • The app's Android manifest does not request the INTERNET permission, so the app cannot send data over the network.
  • Android cloud backup of the app's data is disabled.

4. Spoken instructions

If enabled, instructions may be read aloud by recordings included in the app or by the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. The app only selects voices that work offline. The text-to-speech engine is part of the device's operating system and is governed by its own provider's terms.

5. Sharing, printing and backups — only when a parent chooses

Information leaves the app only when a parent, inside the PIN-protected parent area, chooses to:

  • print a worksheet or report (using the device's print service),
  • share or save a PDF, artwork or a backup file (using the Android share sheet, to a destination the parent selects),
  • restore a backup file the parent selects.

What happens to a file after it is shared is controlled by the parent and the app or service they share it with.

6. Children's privacy & parental control

The app is designed to be set up and controlled by a parent or guardian. Because we do not collect any personal information from children, we do not obtain, hold or process children's data on our systems. Parents can at any time, in the parent area:

  • view a child's learning summary,
  • change or remove a nickname, change the age, or edit preferences,
  • delete a child's profile and all of its data (*Edit profile → Delete profile and data*),
  • delete all app data (*Data & storage → Delete all data*), or uninstall the app, which removes all data from the device.

7. Data retention & security

Data remains on the device until the parent deletes it or uninstalls the app. It is protected by Android's app sandbox; the parent PIN is stored as a salted hash in Android's secure storage. The PIN is a child-resistant gate, not a security guarantee: anyone with full access to an unlocked device may be able to access app data.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information (for example, adding optional cloud backup), we will update this policy and, where required, ask for verifiable parental consent before any change takes effect.
